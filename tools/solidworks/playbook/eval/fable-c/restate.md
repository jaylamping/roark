# C2 restatement (written 06:20, before any C2 modelling)

1. **What the part is for.** The forearm link of a humanoid arm: it carries the wrist joint (a Ø8 pin, fork cheeks) from
   the elbow motor's output flange, 200 mm apart, and takes 60 N at the wrist pin in +X, -X and +Y back to that flange.
2. **What is fixed.** Elbow: the flat ring on Y -4 between Ø30 and Ø62, six Ø3.4 holes on Ø50 (first on +X, 60 degrees
   apart), the Ø30 bore, and a Ø7 x 10 clear cylinder in front of each hole on the Y +4 side. Wrist: pin axis at X 0,
   Z -200 along Y, inner cheek faces flat at Y +-16 over at least Ø16 round the pin, at least 5 mm of material round the
   Ø8 hole, the slot between the cheeks clear from Z -183 downward (a Ø30 sweep about the pin). A straight Ø6 clear
   cable passage from the elbow end to the wrist end, not hanging outside the form.
3. **What good means.** The look first: one continuous body that reads as a limb, not as plates. Then the gate: factor
   at least 2.0 on von Mises (45 MPa), tension across layers (22.5 MPa) and shear between layers (13 MPa) in the print
   direction chosen, mass no more than the boxy link's 261.1 g, one body, watertight, printable in one piece on a
   256 mm bed with no enclosed cavity.
4. **What is mine to choose.** The section and how it changes along the link, the taper, how the disc and the fork grow
   out of the shaft, where the cable runs, the print direction, where material is left out.
5. **Time.** 100 minutes for C1 and C2 from 06:13; stages: C1 to 06:20, ideas and first checkpoint to 06:40, shape to
   07:05, detail and fixed-item checks to 07:20, study to 07:38, report to 07:50.

**Organic, for this part:** the section changes continuously from one end to the other, so the flat elbow disc swells
into an oval shaft and the shaft spreads into the two cheeks the way a bone spreads into its condyles, with no place
where one shape stops and another is attached.

**Three things I will not do:**
1. Round the edges of the boxy link and call it organic (fillets on plates are still plates).
2. Add anything decorative that carries no load and guides no cable: no ribs, fins, lightening windows or surface pattern.
3. Buy the look with the gate: no fixed face moved, no factor under 2.0, no mass above the boxy link.

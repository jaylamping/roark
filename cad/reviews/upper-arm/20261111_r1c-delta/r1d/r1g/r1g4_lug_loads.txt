LOAD CASES (applied to the arm as a remote load at the RS02 centre X 170.49, Y 209.861, Z 5.986; hub top fixed)
- YAW -17 (LC2M): moment -17 N.m about +Y (the RS02's yaw peak, the sign that presses the -X lug into its front plate); no force.
- G90: force -62 N in Z, moment +17.2 N.m about +X (arm horizontal; the forearm and hand load). Carried to the disc face:
  Mx = 17.2 + 62 N x 92.1 mm = 22.91 N.m.
- G90 + YAW -17 (G90YM): both together.

HOW THE FORCES WERE INTEGRATED
- Face rows: hub-side nodal stresses, traction t = sigma . n (n = the hub's outward normal), interpolated linearly over each face and
  integrated on a 0.1 mm grid. Z faces over the flat lug face (X offset 18.76-34.4, Y 288-294.8, less the Dia 3.4 hole); outer face
  over Z 1-11, Y 288-294.8; bolt-hole wall over the cylinder. The force is what OUR ARM APPLIES TO THE HUB.
  Centre of pressure = the compressive-pressure-weighted centre on each Z face. "p mean / p max" = the normal pressure over the
  loaded part and its nodal peak (MPa).
- Section rows: the stress integrated over a Y 294.5 cut through each lug (interior nodes, not extrapolated surface values). They
  must cancel the face forces below the cut; the sign is opposite. The difference measures the nodal over-read of the face rows.
- NO over-count correction is applied to the face rows. The -X lug faces close to their section within 5 %; the +X lug faces read
  16-17 % high (the back-face edge peak, 115-133 MPa nodal). USE THE SECTION TOTALS PER LUG; use the face rows for the split between
  faces and the centres of pressure.
- Whole-hub section at Y 310 (above the disc): everything the arm puts into the hub. Balance: applied vs that section. Moments close
  within 2-3 %, Fz within 10 %. Its Fy reads -9 to -41 N where 0 is applied: integration error on the hub's coarse 4 mm mesh at
  Y 310 (bending stresses that should cancel). The "ring, ridge, groove and spigot" share = applied minus the lugs (sections).
